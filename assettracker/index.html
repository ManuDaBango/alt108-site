Demo · Asset tracker

Let the people who see your assets keep the register up to date.

A waste management company's register is missing hundreds of skip and bulk bins worth about $10,000 each. Updating it is nobody's job. This app makes reporting an asset take seconds.

Try it

Report an asset with the web form and watch it arrive in the register. The review step catches duplicates and errors before they reach the register.
