Demo · Game of Frueets

Too complex to build? Not any more.

A 1960s mechanical slot machine, converted into a multiplayer game you play with friends. We build apps that only exist in your imagination today.

Play Game of Frueets ↗
Time to build 1 day A year ago, a game like this needed a team and took months.

How to play

Press the lever and drop the ball into the same fruit slot 3 times.

3 bells pay the most and the star is a wild card. Not all fruits are equal: the payout list is under the menu button.

Why a game is on a business website

Complex logic

A rules engine
