Proof that "simple apps" don't have to be simple.

I rebuilt Dussar, a card game from my childhood in India, as a four-player web game. It taught me how real software is designed: game rules, turn order and keeping track of every card in play.

Why a card game is on a business website

Complex logic

Rules engine

The game enforces its own rules. Business apps do the same with approvals, limits and checks.

Built end to end
