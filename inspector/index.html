Demo · Inspection reports

Fill in a site report once, on any device. Never re-type it.

An electrical contractor's team filled in Word reports on tablets. Someone then re-typed the answers into another system, which was slow and caused errors. This app replaced the Word form.

Try it

Choose Technician, start the scheduled inspection, fill in a few switchboards and sign off. Your submission is sent to the demo database on the right. Switch to Admin to see job scheduling, switchboard history and the defect register.

This is a demo with made-up data. Please don't enter anything private.

What this could do for your business

Any form can become an app

Word, PDF, paper or spreadsheet. If your team fills it in, it can work like this.

The data starts working for you

Once answers sit in a database they can send an email, build a report or flag a problem on their own.

History you can trust

Every visit is kept. You can see what changed on each switchboard, and when.
