What happens behind the scenes

Nobody has to open the register. The automation does the boring part, around the clock.

  1. A photo comes in by WhatsApp, Telegram, email or the web form. No app to download.
  2. AI reads the photo and pulls out the asset number from the label, barcode or tag.
  3. n8n checks the register to see if the asset is new, moved or a duplicate.
  4. A person approves it with one tap, and the database updates.
  5. Reports go out on their own: an Excel report for the office, a Telegram alert for the manager.
