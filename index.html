After 20 years as a financial analyst, I believe 90% of operational waste isn't decision-making. It's the four jobs around it:

  1. Capturing dataTyping the same details into forms and emails.
  2. Comparing dataReconciling two lists that should match.
  3. Approving exceptionsChasing sign-offs by phone and email.
  4. Updating another systemRe-entering what a computer could copy.

If your team does any of these by hand, there's an app waiting to be built.
